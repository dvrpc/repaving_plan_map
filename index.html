PennDOT District 6: 5-year Repaving Plan (2024-2028)

ABOUT
This map shows PennDOT-owned roads on District 6's resurfacing plan for the next few years. The plan is dynamic, changing based on road conditions, budgets, and other maintenance priorities. This layer is updated as new plans are made available.

The Paving Packages layer shows roads that were on contractor paving projects dating back to 2018.

Layers can be toggled on and off by clicking the layer name in the legend.
